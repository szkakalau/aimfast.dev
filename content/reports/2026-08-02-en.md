**Why not the other two directions**:

**Pricing**: $2.99 one-time (Google Play listing) + $0.99 in-app purchase to remove ads. Consumer pricing, not B2B's $19/month.

2. Upload the APK to Reddit r/androidapps with the title "Permissionless file manager with APK extraction + junk cleanup."
3. If you get 50 test users within 48 hours, spend a week completing the Google Play listing.
